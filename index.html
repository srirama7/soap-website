Our Heritage & Mission

The Story Behind Ayushree Herbals

Ayushree Herbals was founded with a singular, heartfelt vision: to reconnect modern skincare with the pure, time-tested healing wisdom of nature.

In an era dominated by synthetic additives and harsh chemicals, we wanted to create daily essentials that nurture your skin naturally. Every product in our collection—from our soothing Ayukid soap for little ones, radiant Looks beauty bar, therapeutic Ayuderm relief, authentic Panchagavya formulation, to our biodegradable Herbal Detergent—is made with unconditional passion for purity.

Based in Bengaluru, we take pride in delivering trusted, quality herbal solutions that nourish your family with safety and love.
